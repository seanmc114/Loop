🎙️ Allow microphone

Safari on iPhone

  1. Open Settings on your phone
  2. Scroll down to Safari
  3. Tap Microphone → set to Allow
  4. Come back here and tap Record again

Chrome on iPhone

  1. Open Settings on your phone
  2. Scroll down to Chrome
  3. Tap Microphone → turn it On
  4. Come back here and tap Record again

Android

  1. Open Settings
  2. Tap Apps → find your browser
  3. Tap Permissions → Microphone → Allow
  4. Come back here and tap Record again

Or tap the 🔒 icon in your browser address bar and allow microphone there.
